# Sample Refund Policy

This fictional policy is for testing InsightHub knowledge-base search. It is
not a real company policy.

## Refund window

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the original
purchase.

## Eligibility

To be eligible, the purchase must be less than 30 days old. Refunds are
returned to the original payment method. Processing may take 5 to 10 business
days after approval.

## How to request a refund

Contact the support team with the order number and the reason for the request.
The support team will review the request and email the customer with the
decision.
